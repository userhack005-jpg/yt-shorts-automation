Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

SilentVision is a personal Desktop uploader for one YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

When the channel owner signs in, the app requests:

  • YouTube upload, to post Shorts to the SilentVision channel
  • YouTube read, to list that channel's videos
  • YouTube Analytics read, to write a weekly progress report

How that data is used

Google user data is used only to upload videos to SilentVision and to read stats for that same channel. The app does not use Google data to train models, serve ads, or build profiles of other people.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored as GitHub Actions secrets and in a local .env file on the owner's computer. Videos are stored on YouTube. The app does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties.

Retention

Tokens stay until the owner revokes access in their Google account or replaces them. Posted videos stay on YouTube until the owner deletes them.
